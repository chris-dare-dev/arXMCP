## Drift from brief (deliberate)

1. **`infra/cron/` → `ops/cron/`.** The brief says
   `infra/cron/`; the project's established convention from
   E11_S02/E11_S04/E11_S05 is `ops/cron/<name>.sh` for wrappers
   and `ops/systemd/<name>.{service,timer}` for unit pairs. Both
   researchers independently confirmed `infra/cron/` is not a
   real path in the repo. Resolution per D1: shipped at
   `ops/cron/` + `ops/systemd/`.

2. **Authored `tools/parser_failures_report.py`** even though the
   brief asserts it was "authored in E02_S06." E02 stops at S05;
   no such milestone or script exists. Scope expansion per D2.
   The script reads both the TSV format used by E02 producers
   (chunker/preamble/embedder/seed-fetcher) and the JSONL format
   used by E11 producers (oai_delta/bulk_ingest/re_embed). ~250
   LOC.

3. **Note 08 cadence vs shipped cadence.** The note's 00:00 UTC
   delta is aspirational; production runs at 02:00 UTC. The new
   `docs/ops/daily-ops-cadence.md` documents the shipped times
   and flags the divergence in a sidebar. Rewriting note 08 is
   out of scope.

4. **The brief asked for ".timer files"** as the only systemd
   deliverable per cron. systemd `.timer` files are not
   executable on their own — they trigger a paired `.service`.
   Ship both per E11 precedent. Per D6.

5. **E11_S04 watchdog systemd parity fill.** The E11_S04 cron
   wrapper shipped without a systemd unit pair (only delta and
   backup got one). E14_S04 lands the missing
   `arxmcp-watchdog.{timer,service}` while it's here.

6. **Phoenix Prometheus scrape line dropped.** The brief asserts
   the Phoenix profile "starts a Prometheus scrape target."
   Phoenix is not a scraper (factually wrong claim caught in
   E14_S03). Out of scope here; Grafana + Prometheus profile is
   E14_S09.

## What this milestone does NOT cover

- **Ingestion-throughput metrics.** Note 08 names
  `arxmcp_ingest_papers_processed_total` and
  `arxmcp_ingest_chunks_written_total` but no emitter exists in
  the codebase. The daily report renders a placeholder for that
  section.
- **Grafana alerting rules.** The thresholds documented in
  `docs/ops/daily-ops-cadence.md` are operator references; the
  Grafana profile + alert rules land in E14_S09.
- **Note 08 rewrite.** Cadence drift documented but the note
  itself is unchanged.
- **Email send-loop hardening.** STARTTLS opt-in + SMTP auth opt-
  in are both supported, but a production deployment with
  external SMTP relay would want certificate verification + a
  retry loop. Future hardening.
- **Phoenix retrieval-quality reports.** That's E14_S03's
  surface; not consumed by the daily report.
